This directory contains simulation files where hv11 interacts with ttr. Three trial were named as below.

dim - Simulation files where hv11 binds to dimer-dimer interface at the begining of the simulation. Starting conformation was obtained by docking hv11 specifically into dimer-dimer interface of ttr.
mon - Simulation files where hv11 binds to 1-2 monomer-monomer interface at the begining of the simulation. Starting conformation was obtained by docking hv11 specifically into 1-2 monomer-monomer interface.
random - Simulation files where hv11 binds to 3-4 monomer-monomer interface at the begining of the simulation. Starting conformation was obtained by performing docking calculations assigning the active residues randomly spread over the entire TTR surface.
